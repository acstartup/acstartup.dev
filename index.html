Cambridge, MA Python · Pytest · PyPI · LaTeX · Figma

Researcher & publication co-author. TaintMonkey is a dynamic taint analysis library for Python Flask apps that uses monkey patching to instrument endpoints without changing source code. It includes a built-in fuzzer and plugin workflow to generate randomized inputs and detect vulnerabilities when tainted data reaches dangerous sinks without proper sanitization. The repo also ships with JungleGym, a CWE-labeled benchmark dataset of 118 vulnerable Flask apps.

The project began at NJ GSET 2025 and was later presented at MIT URTC 2025 and published in IEEE Xplore. I built 32 of the 118 JungleGym apps along with their vulnerability plugins, added JSON-input tainting support, fixed major GitHub issues affecting data-flow tracking, and contributed to debugging and core architectural decisions. I also wrote the Experimental Procedures section of the paper, designed all research figures in Figma, formatted the manuscript in Overleaf, and created the 13-slide deck presented at MIT URTC 2025. Throughout, I coordinated deadlines and kept the team on pace.
